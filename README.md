### OpenAI Devs — official video showcase (seed)

[OpenAI Devs](https://x.com/openaidevs) · [status/2098827327832822014](https://x.com/openaidevs/status/2098827327832822014)

Public multi-video showcase. **Do not rehost the media.** Use as visual reference for pacing / hook / proof, then remake with *your* product.

**Remake prompt (ours — not OpenAI’s):**

```text
You are remaking a winning short ad structure, not inventing a new category.

Inputs:
- Reference: the OpenAI Devs showcase thread https://x.com/openaidevs/status/2098827327832822014 (watch only; do not copy branded assets)
- My product: [SKU / pack shot / URL]
- My offer: [one-line offer]
- My proof: [testimonial / demo / metric I actually have]

Tasks:
1) List hook, pacing beats, proof moment, and CTA from the reference structure (no invented claims).
2) Write a shot list that keeps my product on-camera in every hero beat.
3) Produce still frames for the hero product moments, then a remake script for a 15–30s ad.
4) End with one clear CTA to my offer — not a studio/cinema pitch.
```

### Decode a winner before you remake it

EasyVeo · Analyzer-first

```text
I have an authorized short ad clip (MP4/WebM, ≤30s). Decode it into ~18 structural elements:
hook, pacing, proof, CTA, and supporting beats.

Rules:
- Private by default; do not invent competitor ROAS or “will go viral” claims.
- Output a remake brief: what to keep vs rewrite for MY product.
- Then suggest stills + video remake order (stills before long-form).
```
